# Physics and Mathematics Book Solutions

A collection of worked solutions, computational explorations, and study notes based on a selection of undergraduate and graduate textbooks in physics and mathematics.

The goals of this repository are to

- develop a deeper understanding of fundamental concepts,
- produce carefully written and reproducible solutions,
- explore analytical and computational approaches to physical and mathematical problems,
- document interesting derivations and insights,
- and build a long-term, publicly available collection of high-quality learning material.

Most handwritten work is produced in Xournal++ and exported to PDF. Where appropriate, accompanying Python or R code is included to verify calculations, perform simulations, or investigate extensions of textbook problems.

# Repository Philosophy

Reading mathematics and physics is valuable, but genuine understanding comes from solving problems.

This repository therefore focuses on

- deriving results from first principles,
- understanding the intuition behind mathematical and physical ideas,
- comparing different solution methods,
- verifying results computationally whenever appropriate,
- and writing solutions that are clear, complete, and reproducible.

Whenever possible, analytical work is complemented by numerical experiments or simulations.

---

# Computational Work

Many chapters include Python or R scripts.

These scripts may

- verify analytical derivations,
- solve problems numerically,
- generate figures,
- simulate physical systems,
- investigate limiting cases,
- or explore ideas beyond those covered in the textbook.

The aim is not merely to obtain the correct answer, but to better understand the underlying mathematics and physics.
